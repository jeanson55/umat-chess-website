About the Club

A university community for strategy, learning, friendly competition and personal growth.

Our purpose

The UMaT Chess & Scrabble Club brings students together around games that reward concentration, planning, creativity and sportsmanship. The supplied programme welcomes beginners and returning players and combines coaching with practical play.

How we learn

Members can develop through demonstrations, guided exercises, puzzles, friendly games and review. The current draft programme is focused on chess, with Scrabble activities separately listed for April–July 2027.
