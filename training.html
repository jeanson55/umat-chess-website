Coaching & Training

A practical pathway from chess fundamentals to confident tournament play.

The training topics below are adapted from the club's draft ten-week programme. Sessions meet at CL1; check the club's WhatsApp group for current session times.

01

Build the foundations

Piece movement, opening principles, centre control, development, king safety and sound habits.

02

Train tactical vision

Puzzles and guided exercises on forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks and mating patterns.

03

Calculate with purpose

Practise checks, captures, threats, candidate moves and blunder checks before committing.

04

Understand positional play

Explore pawn structures, weak squares, outposts and open files through examples and games.

05

Improve endgames

Study king-and-pawn endings, opposition, rule of the square and rook-endgame technique.

06

Play, review, improve

Practical games, clock management, notation, etiquette and reflection on performance.
